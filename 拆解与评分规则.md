# Teardown And Scoring

Use these formats after confirming what parts of the video are actually observable. Adapt the amount of detail to the source, but do not omit evidence labels.

## 1. Original video information card

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Duration | Exact duration, or unknown |
| Main language | Spoken or written language |
| Video type | Product demo, factory, test, story, founder talk, case study, problem solving, etc. |
| Likely audience | Buyer role, industry, region, and interest level; mark as inferred |
| Product or topic | Exact subject visible |
| Main scenes | Locations and production contexts |
| On-camera presenter | Yes, no, hands-only, voice-over only |
| Voice-over or speech | Yes, no, partial |
| Background music | Yes, no, useful character |
| Subtitles | Languages, placement, and density |
| Main CTA | Exact action requested |
| Performance evidence | Available platform metrics or no verified data |

If the viewer count, retention, engagement, inquiry, or conversion data is unavailable, state that the “爆款” label is unverified.

## 2. First three seconds

Break the opening into meaningful visual or audio changes. Use half-second ranges only when the source supports that precision; otherwise use approximate ranges and label them.

| Element | Questions to answer |
| --- | --- |
| First frame | What is seen first? Is the subject a person, product, process, result, or text? Is motion present? |
| First spoken line | What exactly is said? Is it a question, result, conflict, benefit, identity cue, proof, or counterintuitive claim? |
| Visual-audio sync | Do image and sound create attention together? Does the subtitle reinforce the spoken claim? |
| New information by second three | What changes by second three? Is there a reveal, action, proof, or unresolved result? |
| Buyer relevance | Is this likely to attract a real procurement buyer or mainly general curiosity? |
| Transferability | Which hook and visual mechanism could work for the user's B2B category, and under what conditions? |

Finish with a specific formula:

```text
Hook mechanism + opening visual + evidence/reveal + buyer relevance + conditions
```

Do not write only “create suspense.” Identify what is unknown, what image creates it, and which audience it targets.

## 3. Shot timeline

Record every meaningful shot change when it can be observed.

| Time range | Speech or subtitle meaning | Visible action and scene | Camera and subject movement | Edit treatment | Distribution role | Commercial role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

For every row:

- Keep the actual action, person, product, and environment.
- Describe how sound, speech, text, and image work together.
- Mark whether the shot adds new information or repeats an earlier point.
- Label core shots and optional shots.
- Mark unclear cuts and approximate ranges as `REVIEW`.
- Do not invent a cut or timestamp that cannot be verified.

## 4. Narrative and visual structure

First identify the structure actually used. Possible structures include:

- story narrative
- product demonstration
- manufacturing or process
- experiment or test
- before/after comparison
- procurement risk or buying advice
- factory capability
- founder direct-to-camera
- customer case
- problem solving

Then explain:

- why this line or shot comes first
- why the next segment follows it
- how information escalates
- which lines carry commercial value
- which lines could be removed without breaking logic
- whether the ending naturally leads to the CTA

Output a compact `原视频结构骨架` with opening, progression, proof, result, and CTA.

## 5. Cinematography reverse engineering

Analyze:

1. Camera position and angle
2. Wide, medium, close-up, or detail framing
3. Fixed, push, pan, tracking, or handheld movement
4. Subject placement and composition
5. Presenter expression and gesture
6. Product reveal order
7. Light direction, softness, color, and contrast
8. Scene credibility
9. Relationship between camera movement and emotional intent
10. How an ordinary operator could reproduce the shot with a phone

For important shots state where the operator stands, what is in frame, how the phone moves, and how many seconds to capture.

## 6. Editing reverse engineering

Analyze only what can be observed:

- opening and middle pacing
- long and short shot durations
- information density and likely drop-off points
- cuts following meaning, action, speech, or music
- unnatural pauses, posture jumps, or broken sentences
- zooms, speed changes, hard cuts, transitions, and effects
- B-roll used as evidence
- subtitle typography, position, density, emphasis, and language
- original sound, voice-over clarity, music character, and sound effects

Do not report exact BPM, frame rate, or editing software without evidence.

## 7. Why it may travel

Address each mechanism with evidence:

1. Stop mechanism: why a viewer may stop scrolling
2. Continued-view mechanism: what creates forward movement
3. Curiosity mechanism: what result or answer remains unknown
4. Emotion mechanism: surprise, concern, desire, recognition, etc.
5. Information value: what is learned, seen, or solved
6. Credibility: which observable facts, processes, tests, or materials prove the claim
7. Interaction mechanism: why a viewer may comment, save, send, or share
8. Commercial attraction: why a relevant buyer may care about the product, supply capability, or cooperation
9. Conversion mechanism: how the video naturally leads to DM, WhatsApp, catalog, quote, or project inquiry
10. Weakness: where reach may increase while buyer quality or trust decreases

All unsupported causal claims must be labeled as content-analysis hypotheses.

## 8. Dual 100-point score

Use the following editorial defaults unless the user supplies a custom rubric. Explain the evidence behind each dimension; the score is not platform data.

### Communication power

| Dimension | Default points |
| --- | ---: |
| First three seconds | 25 |
| Visual impact | 15 |
| Story or information structure | 20 |
| Pacing | 15 |
| Emotional pull | 15 |
| Completion potential | 10 |

### Commercial power

| Dimension | Default points |
| --- | ---: |
| Buyer precision | 20 |
| Procurement-problem relevance | 20 |
| Product clarity | 15 |
| Real evidence | 20 |
| Professional trust | 15 |
| CTA and inquiry handoff | 10 |

Conclude whether the video is better suited to broad reach or qualified customer development. If it is weak for B2B acquisition, say why.

## 9. Reusable structure card

Create a `爆款结构母版卡`:

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Transferable industry | Where the structure fits |
| Target buyer | Role and buying context |
| Content objective | Reach, trust, inquiry, etc. |
| Suggested duration | Range and reason |
| Shot order | Reusable visual sequence |
| Information order | Reusable narrative sequence |
| Evidence type | What must be real |
| CTA | Buyer-appropriate next action |
| Reuse risk | Compliance, trust, product, or localization risk |

Extract structure and principles. Do not copy the source's distinctive script, imagery, music, or creator identity.
